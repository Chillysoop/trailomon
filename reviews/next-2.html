Completed: D1–D3 / original proposals

D11. Meet the other starting hauler

Add one optional, habitat-appropriate invitation for whichever hauler Discovery lacks: Brindlehorn or Reedskipper. Show the supply cost, crew space and daily meals before accepting. Keep the companion for future departures.

Why: the starting choice currently makes one of six species unobtainable in that depot. Reuse the existing recruitment, retention and food-preview systems.

Cost: one small content/rules PR; two conditional scenes, no new art. Recommendation: first. Works without D2/D3.

D22. Give the field guide useful clues

Add a short habitat/landmark clue and an encounter-method hint for each existing species. Separate reliable sightings from unconfirmed reports. Use the same location symbols as the route map; keep details in the guide.

Why: unseen entries currently say “Watch wildlife to learn its habits.” Existing notes, observations and route reports provide the structure, but do not tell a player how to plan a search.

Cost: one small content/UI PR. No route redesign or guaranteed rare encounter. Recommendation: second; turns discovery into a reason to choose a route.

D33. Spend a day following tracks

At a suitable stopped stretch, follow one visible wildlife clue. Preview one day, meals and any help required; then resolve a sighting, assistance or invitation using existing encounter rules. Passing remains free.

Why: wildlife now arrives mainly through random travel slots or fixed invitations. A chosen detour adds “do I have time and food to find it?” to the expedition loop.

Cost: one medium gameplay PR. At most one search per stretch, fixed seeded outcome saved before the choice; reloading cannot reroll it. No random walking or farming. Recommendation: third; uses current species and scenery. Useful alone, clearest after D2.

D44. Give Mara a different plan next trip

Add two alternative practice lineups using existing creatures. Show her lineup and its intent before setup. Rotate deterministically between journeys; retain one optional practice per trip, real energy costs and safe practice damage.

Why: Mara remembers previous trips, but currently repeats Brindlehorn/Bellmoth and the same three-stage pattern. Existing encounter intents and profile recognition can support variation.

Cost: one medium combat/content PR plus pair checks. No XP, new trainer roster or battle ladder. Recommendation: later, after the discovery loop.

D55. Give the guide a completion goal

Add two optional journal aims: observe all six species in the wild, and travel with all six across any number of expeditions. Show quiet progress in the guide and a journal entry on completion.

Why: observations and traveled-species records already persist, while the three current aims focus on delivery pairs, paths and peaceful arrival. This adds a collection purpose without permanent power.

Cost: one small PR reusing journal aims. Discovery completion needs D1; do not promise an unreachable goal. Recommendation: after D1–D3; no stat reward or mandatory grind.
