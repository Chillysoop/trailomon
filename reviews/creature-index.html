Trailomon / Authoring ledger / November 1, 2026

A country full of creatures.

A first set of biology-led identities for the approved ability-first direction. No universal type chart. No creature-created terrain effects.

24 briefs / 6 species in game / 3 S28 companion kits / 276 unfilled slots

These are first design briefs, not 24 playable kits. The final target remains at least 300 distinct implemented species. Farther regions are planned; the current game still follows the Fernwake–Lantern route. Art remains deferred.

Decisions recorded: B1/B2/B4/B5/B6/B7/B8 approved. B3 rejected. This is a read-only deliverable; no repeat vote is needed.

TR-001 BrindlehornExisting species / S28 companion kit

Herd grazer and established working companion.

Body
A low, heavy neck carries broad hollow antlers with blunt openings; its weight sits behind the head.
Habitat
Open scrub and grassland margins with enough wind to carry herd calls.
Diet
Coarse grass, tender brush and fallen orchard leaves.
Why it lives this way
Antler chambers carry a low call beyond a rise. Herds keep loose contact without needing a clear view.
Watch for
Stops twice at a ridge: once to listen, once to set its feet.
Encounter
Existing depot companion and grassland wildlife; listen for the answering herd before approaching.

Active: Antler brace

Shields itself and explicitly shields the wagon.

Trigger: Focus full. Bound: Shield cannot restore lost HP or wagon integrity.

Passive: Rooted

Gains defense in a front position.

Trigger: Occupying the front. Bound: The extra defense does not follow it to the rear.

Counterplay: Disrupt its attack clock, attack the uncovered lane or force a formation choice.

Tradeoff: Reliable protection costs the party a faster attacker’s position.

Does: brace, shield, intercept. Responds to: strike. These are actions, not classes.

Ecological links: No named link in this first brief.

Production: Species, art and S28 kit exist. The expanded lore is a brief; S30 must integrate the kit with expedition costs.

Recruitment: existing. Art: existing. Region: Fernwake–Lantern road.

TR-002 ReedskipperExisting species / proposed repeatable kit

Social shallows dweller, swimmer and working companion.

Body
Broad body, webbed feet and a thick tail ending in a flat paddle; the tail changes its direction rather than acting as a sail.
Habitat
Shallow shelves, reed margins and side channels rather than deep open water.
Diet
Water shoots, soft stems and small shellfish found along the bottom.
Why it lives this way
Feels a sideways current through its tail and changes course before its body is swept away.
Watch for
Pushes a floating object aside, then waits for it to drift back.
Encounter
Existing depot companion; Willowford observations and invitations already use its swimming ecology.

Active: Paddle sweep

Interrupts a charging enemy and redirects its next basic strike toward Reedskipper.

Trigger: Focus full; prefer a charging enemy. Bound: Redirects one basic strike, never an entire ability or a wagon-only attack.

Passive: Give with it

After redirecting a hit, advances its own next attack.

Trigger: A redirected hit lands. Bound: One response per redirection; no recursive counters.

Counterplay: Use steady low-pressure attacks instead of telegraphed rushes.

Tradeoff: Redirecting a strong hit puts its own HP at risk.

Does: interrupt, strike. Responds to: charge. These are actions, not classes.

Ecological links: No named link in this first brief.

Production: Existing species and legacy kit; this repeatable kit is proposed for S29. B5 later offers one learned channel or still-pool trick.

Recruitment: existing. Art: existing. Region: Fernwake–Lantern road.

TR-062 BorrowbillNew species brief / not in game

Parasite-picker and potential symbiotic companion.

Body
A narrow hooked bill, short grasping legs and a flattened chest let it lie close against a larger animal’s back.
Habitat
Floodplain herds and the dry margins where grazers gather after rain.
Diet
Ticks, biting flies and loose skin removed from larger hosts.
Why it lives this way
The host gains relief; Borrowbill gains food and a moving lookout. It cannot choose a perch that cannot carry it.
Watch for
Checks the same shoulder fold before settling beneath the host’s mane.
Encounter
A patient herd approach reveals it on a Tallowback. Gaining the host’s calm makes the small animal easier to meet.

Active: Shared perch

Attaches to a willing large ally, giving up its own basic attacks for shared protection.

Trigger: Focus full with a suitable unattached host. Bound: One host and one passenger; detaches when the host falls. No invulnerability or chained hosting.

Passive: Clean coat

While perched, periodically removes one minor disabling effect from its host.

Trigger: A bounded interval on the same living host. Bound: Does not remove every effect at once or refill supplies.

Counterplay: Disrupt or isolate the host; pressure the passenger after separation.

Tradeoff: Two crew slots become one attacking body, a serious cost in a three-creature party.

Does: perch, recover. Responds to: fall. These are actions, not classes.

Ecological links: Tallowback

Production: New design and the bounded B4 symbiosis candidate, scheduled after S29. No dedicated art yet.

Recruitment: planned companion. Art: deferred. Region: The shifting flats.
