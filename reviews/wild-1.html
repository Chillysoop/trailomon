Trailomon · new design choices

Wildlife and fellow crews

More discovery, collecting and creature companionship. Keep the wagon journey at the center. My first picks: W1–W3.

What we already have — and the main limits

Six species, two help-then-recruit stories, habitat/weather events, peaceful wildlife encounters, memories, a journal and automatic combat. Four species already start unlocked.

We store one companion per species. Combat has two player positions against one opposing Resolve track; another fully simulated team would need a larger redesign.

The first batch uses existing art. Human meetings belong near the depots; wildlife takes over farther out. These are new proposals, not implemented features.

W1Wildlife belongs to its habitat

Three sightings: meadow Brindlehorn, woodland Bellmoths, riverside Reedskippers. Watch, help or pass. Familiar places become places to look for creatures.

Fits: Existing habitat checks, event choices and cooldown. Replaces generic ambient opportunities; keeps quiet travel and recruitment priority.

Cost: Small–medium. Three scenes and selection tests. Pick: First.

W2A field guide worth filling

For each species: Known / Seen wild / Traveled with, plus one learned behavior. A sighting matters even when the creature never joins.

Fits: Existing discoveries, Journal and journey endings. Also records current wildlife encounters; does not require W1.

Cost: Medium. New observation records and a compact species view. No XP grind. Pick: First.

W3Spar with a courier’s crew

One optional Mossbell match against a Brindlehorn/Bellmoth pair. Protect a practice load. Spend real energy; keep delivery cargo and companion condition safe. Skip freely.

Fits: Our positions, cover, creature abilities and automatic exchanges. Prototype two visible opposing roles with one shared Resolve track, not a fully simulated second team.

Cost: Medium–large. New opposing effects, practice rules, forecasts and saves. One attempt per run; no farmable rewards. Pick: After W1/W2.

W4A traveler who remembers you

Meet one courier twice. Their later greeting reflects your earlier choice. If W3 is selected, use the same courier.

Fits: Existing event follow-ups and memories. Adds a recurring friend/rival without a league or mandatory race.

Cost: Small–medium. Two scenes and saved recognition. Pick: After the first batch.

W5Start small, find your crew

An optional new-profile mode: choose one hauler, then befriend Bellmoth and Loamling on the road. Keep normal starts and existing saves intact.

Fits: Recruitment and food previews. Currently four species are available immediately, limiting the thrill of acquiring them.

Cost: Large. New recruitment paths and starting-economy balance. Pick: Later, if collecting should become central.

W6Companions learn a habit

A journey feat unlocks one selectable travel habit with a tradeoff. Named companions become different through shared experience.

Fits: Memories and the depot. Requires new mechanics; our current trust counter does not provide this.

Cost: Large. Persistent choices, equipment interactions and balance. Pick: Defer.

First playable slice and boundaries

Build W1’s sightings and W2’s observations first, then W3’s single spar. Use six existing species and current art. Keep the three-companion limit, real food/energy tradeoffs and peaceful options.

No extra regions, forced battles, capture devices, elemental chart or battle XP. Full enemy teams and duplicate-creature collecting need separate design. A YES approves that bounded option; a note can narrow it.
