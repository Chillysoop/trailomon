The plan we are building toward

One traveler, a wagon, and a small crew of original creatures venture farther from dependable help. Prepare supplies, read the country, choose a route, and decide how to spend time and the crew’s energy. Companions help with work and short automatic encounters. Discoveries and shared history give the next journey a different starting point.

  • One route, six landmarks, six species, and two encounter scenarios.
  • Calm travel, consequential stops, and a target of 20–35 minutes per expedition.
  • Deliberate pixel art, distinct creature silhouettes, and consistent labeled icons.
  • New regions come after this expedition works well. Public release publication requires your approval.
