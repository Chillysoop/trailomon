# Creature scope and identity

2026-11-01: Owner requires a creature index with at least 300 species. This means actual distinct species as the intended game target, not 300 classes. Six currently exist in both code and lore. Track future designed/implemented/art-ready counts honestly. Broader scope is a long-term expansion of the first-expedition prototype.

2026-11-01: Species must relate to the environment where they live. They need not all be useful travelers: owner explicitly includes combat, food, protection and speed. Preserve room for predators, prey, domestic animals and odd wildlife. No mandatory utility or job for every species.

2026-11-01: Owner floated abandoning types entirely in favor of individual abilities and asked for creative alternatives. The suggested twelve-class scheme was withdrawn before publication. No type/archetype roster is selected. See docs/CREATURE_DIRECTION.md and bestiary-1 B1–B8 for proposals. Habitat influence is approved as a creative requirement; new terrain mechanics, migration, hunting and social attachment remain separate proposals.

S28 remains authorized while this direction is reviewed; its three original kits require no type chart. B1/B2/B8 are the review's recommended foundation; other ideas are bounded later experiments. No new owner votes have been created.
